# Interactive bazaar (Phase 3A)

Six *decorative* stalls share a typed, stable-ID configuration in `frontend/src/world/bazaar.ts`. The identifiers `silk-001`, `diya-001`, `flowers-001`, `food-001`, `brass-001`, `sweets-001` are scene identifiers, **not** bookable rental records. Goods and INR prices are illustrative only; no inventory, purchase, payment or vendor account exists.

The existing ghat runs across x = −21…21 with the Ganga toward negative z. Stalls sit on the z = 15…18 upper terrace, arranged across x; the opening between the central stalls and the clear aisle at z ≈ 14.5 connect them to the river path. An elevated entrance sign, fabric canopies, stall-specific goods, stylized vendor figures, garlands and festoon lights mark the market. Goods are original procedural geometry; sign textures are generated locally with Canvas and disposed on unmount. The `BazaarScene.tsx` market, `StallGoods.tsx` displays and `StallPanel.tsx` native HTML dialog keep geometry and interface separate.

`environment.ts` rejects movement into the six stall bodies while preserving the riverfront boundary and existing pillar collisions. The follow camera shifts to the open aisle when its desired location enters a stall footprint; other decorative geometry is not raycast. `nearestStall` selects exactly one nearby interaction point, using distance and configuration order for ties. A single E handler in `App.tsx` opens the nearest stall; a touch-friendly prompt provides the same action. While the dialog is open movement is paused, native focus stays in the dialog, and Escape or a close control returns to exploration. Focused controls do not trigger movement keys.

Low graphics halves the festoon/flower instances, reduces the display details, omits vendor facial details and market point lighting. High quality retains one small market light and existing shadow settings. The scene is memoized so five-per-second HUD position updates do not rebuild the 3D market. No new runtime dependencies or remote assets were added.

Known limitations: collider rectangles are approximations rather than mesh-accurate physics; tall structures may still obscure the camera at some angles. The illustrative products are not interactable individually. The rendered desktop and touch captures are produced by Playwright in a software-rendered browser: [desktop bazaar](bazaar-desktop.png) and [mobile bazaar](bazaar-mobile.png). Real-device frame rates, screen-reader alternatives to free 3D navigation and genuine commercial data remain unverified.
