## Original project synthesis

`sandhya-drone.wav`, `deep-ki-lau.wav`, `river-loop.wav`, `crowd-loop.wav`,
`bells-loop.wav`, `spark-fountain.wav`, `gift-feedback.wav`, `offering-water.wav`
are generated in this project using elementary oscillators and deterministic noise.
Source: `scripts/prepare_phase5_audio.py` in the project release. No external sample,
song, lyric, voice, artist imitation or field recording is used.

These generated audio assets are provided under **CC0 1.0**, to the extent rights
exist and may be waived: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
They may be bundled and reproduced without required attribution. This statement
does not claim copyright necessarily exists in generated output in every jurisdiction.

The music is an original meditative/drone-style composition, not a traditional
performance, licensed bhajan or authentic priest/Aarti recording. The crowd is an
abstract noise texture, not intelligible human speech. Synthesized bells/river are
not recordings of actual Banaras locations. The historical demo chime remains
labelled as a demo and is not used as the finished radio programme.
